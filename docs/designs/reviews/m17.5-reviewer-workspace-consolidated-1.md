## Escalations

| # | Finding | Disposition | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | The reviewer could walk to the author's folder, because the two are siblings under one managed root and the gateway documents a write boundary rather than a read boundary | **ESCALATE** | the gateway owns the folder layout: one managed root, and a worker's permissions. GAOS can give the reviewer its own root with its own standing files, which is what this change does; it cannot give the reviewer an OS-level boundary from the author's folder. That is the gateway's workspace model, and it is the open question M17 recorded |
| E2 | The transcript's physical location is not documented — only that it is keyed by the workspace id | **ESCALATE** | a distinct id means a distinct key; the claim is stated no stronger than that |

## Nothing was contested

No finding was contested. Every one of them was either a fact about the repository that the
document had to carry, or a defect in a document whose subject is facts. The two dispositions above
are escalations, not contests: the finding was right, and the fix belongs to another change.
